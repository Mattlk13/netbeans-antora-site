The Apache NetBeans team is pleased to announce that Apache NetBeans 30 was released on May 18, 2026.

Apache NetBeans is a Java-based IDE, Tooling Platform and Application Framework.

This is the second release of 2026 in our regular quarterly release schedule.

Community provided installers are listed on the main download page.

What's in the Apache NetBeans 30 release:

https://github.com/apache/netbeans/releases/tag/30

With thanks to 20 contributors, including 1 who have contributed for the first time. Thank you!
